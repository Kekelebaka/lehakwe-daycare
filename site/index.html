About Us

Where township children thrive

Lehakwe Daycare brings modern early childhood development to Bloemside. We combine play-based learning with qualified care to give your child the strongest start. Nutritious meals, stimulating activities, and a safe space — all at an affordable rate. Because every child in the township deserves world-class care.
